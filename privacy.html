Privacy Policy - DD Compressor

Last updated: September 15, 2026

DD Compressor is designed as a privacy-first file compression application. This Privacy Policy explains how the app handles files, permissions, and information.

1. File Processing

DD Compressor is designed to process selected files locally on your device. Your selected files are not intentionally uploaded to a DD Compressor server for compression or storage.

2. Files and Personal Content

The app may access files that you explicitly select for compression. These files may contain personal or sensitive information. The app uses access to the selected files only to perform the requested operation and provide the compressed result.

3. Storage and Downloads

When you choose to save or download a compressed file, the app may request the necessary device permission or use the operating system's file-saving mechanism. You control which files you select and where supported files are saved.

4. Data Collection

DD Compressor does not intentionally collect, sell, or rent the contents of your files. The app is designed to avoid unnecessary collection of personal file data.

5. Third-Party Services

DD Compressor is designed to minimize unnecessary third-party data transfer. If a future version introduces a third-party service that materially changes how user data is handled, this Privacy Policy will be updated before or with that change where applicable.

6. Security

Reasonable measures are used to keep file processing local and minimize unnecessary exposure of user data. However, no software can guarantee absolute security, so users should avoid selecting files they do not want an application to process.

7. Children's Privacy

DD Compressor does not intentionally collect personal information from children. The app is a general-purpose utility and should be used in accordance with applicable laws and platform requirements.

8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app's features or data practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.

9. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or DD Compressor's privacy practices, please use the contact information provided on the app's official store listing.
